## MOE license support extended for 2022-2023

We are pleased to announce that OARC and the Office for Research reached the agreement to continue support for MOE license for the year 2022-2023. After this year, the management of the license, including the cost and hosting of the license server will be transferred entirely to the Office for Research. OARC will continue the technical support of MOE on the OARC clusters and assist MOE users with scientific consultations. Please contact us at help@oarc.rutgers.edu if you have any questions.

Thanks, Vlad Kholodovych, PhD Sr Scientist,Research Support Office of Advanced Research Computing (OARC)
