## OARC's feature in the Rutgers IT Annual Report

Hello, Amarel users!

We are pleased to announce that the [Rutgers IT 2022-2023 Annual Report](https://it.rutgers.edu/annual-review-2022-2023/) was released, and it highlights many of the Office of Advanced Research Computing ([OARC](http://oarc.rutgers.edu/))'s impressive accomplishments over the past fiscal year. We are proud to be able to provide services and support to help you perform life-changing research.

Below is a summary of OARC's achievements featured in the report:

- Amarel has surpassed 2 BILLION+ compute hours, with over 4,800 Rutgers faculty, staff, and
 students putting it to work for research, training, and education purposes.
- 365+ Rutgers departments, institutes, and organizations perform research on systems maintained by
 OARC.
- $66.6+ million dollars in new research awards for OARC-partnered projects since March 2016.
- 1800+ faculty, staff, and students from 200+ departments across Rutgers campuses have attended
 high performance computing training since inception in 2017.

We encourage you to take a moment and reflect with us by looking through this year's Rutgers IT annual review, a digital publication that highlights notable data and information about collaborative projects of various technology teams across the university.
