## Caliburn allocations and submission process

**CALIBURN 2021 SESSIONS ARE IN PROGRESS!**

Twice per year (spring and fall), the Rutgers Office of Advanced Research Computing (OARC) invites applications for the allocation of computing resources on Caliburn - New Jersey's largest academic supercomputing facility - which provides high-performance computing capabilities to academic researchers across the state to accelerate research programs that use or develop highly scalable computing applications.

The current awarded allocations are active from 1/1/2021 - 6/30/2021.

The Caliburn allocations application can be found [here](https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=caliburn-2021-1). Instructions can be viewed here: Call for Proposals: [Caliburn Allocation Requests [PDF]](https://oarc.rutgers.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2020/10/OARC_Caliburn_CFP_Nov.2020_v2.pdf).

In addition to applications for awarded allocations, startup allocations are also welcome. Startup applications are provided as a means to have full access with a limited-time usage allocation; they can be converted into awarded allocations during the next call for proposal cycle. Applications from any academic/research institution in New Jersey are welcome.

**We would like to inform current awardees that current research awards will expire on June 30th, 2021.**

Email [help@oarc.rutgers.edu](mailto:help@oarc.rutgers.edu) with any questions regarding Caliburn.
