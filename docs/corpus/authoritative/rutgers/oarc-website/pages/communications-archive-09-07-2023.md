## Network interruption and possible job failure

Dear Amarel Users, We had a network outage an hour ago and our tech team resolved this outage in the next 30 mins or so. During these outage period, a few compute nodes were off-lined and the jobs running on these compute nodes got cancelled. As of now, these nodes are back to production. Please double check the completion status of your jobs and resubmit them if needed. Bala Bala Desinghu, PhD Senior Scientist Office of Advanced Research Computing (OARC)
