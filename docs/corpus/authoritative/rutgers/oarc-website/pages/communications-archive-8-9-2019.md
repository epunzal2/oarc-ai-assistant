## Newark Data Center Maintenance

Ehud (Udi) Zelzion Friday, August 9, 2019 at 10:02 am

OARC Research Computing Users,

Due to maintenance work that will take place between **9pm Thursday Aug 22 to 5pm Friday Aug 23** at the **Newark Data Center**, all Newark-based systems will be powered-down and unavailable.

If you plane on submitting jobs targeting Newark-based resources (e.g., Amarel-N and 'nm3' partitions) please make sure that your job's run-time doesn't overlap with the maintenance window, which will cause your jobs to stay "Pending" and won't start.

Please let us know if you have questions or need help.

Ehud (Udi) Zelzion, Senior Scientist Office of Advanced Research Computing (OARC) and Genome Cooperative @SEBS
