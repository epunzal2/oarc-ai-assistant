## Unexpected network problems

As part of our scheduled four-year cycle for equipment renewal, we plan to update our GSS storage system at Piscataway/NB campus. This change will only impact the /scratch space; individual /home directories and /projects spaces will not be affected.

As a result, all files in /scratch will be removed. To lessen the disruption to your work, we are implementing a phased approach where users can transfer important files from /scratch to a new temporary location that will eventually become the new /scratch.

Please remember that the OARC staff won't assist in this transfer, so it's up to the users to move any files they think will be necessary.

Here is the planned schedule:

- March 1st, 2024: A new temporary space /newscratch has become available for users to transfer files from /scratch.

- April 16th, 2024, during April's maintenance: The temporary space /newscratch will become /scratch, and the old scratch known as /scratch before April 16, will be renamed to /oldscratch. This new /oldscratch space will be accessible as a read-only location for another month, until maintenance in May.

- May 19th, 2024, during May's maintenance: The old scratch /oldscratch will be discontinued.
