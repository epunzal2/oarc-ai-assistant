## Reminder: Amarel will be Offline for Maintenance JAN 11-15

Friday, January 8, 11:31 am

Dear OARC Users,

A quick reminder that Amarel will be offline for maintenance during Jan 11 - Jan 15. I am forwarding the initial announcement (see below my signature) that has more details about the upcoming maintenance.

Please contact us if you have any questions or need any help.

Thank you Bala

Tuesday, December 22, 2020 at 10:31 am

Amarel Users,

The entire Amarel system (all campuses) will be offline for routine maintenance starting at 10:00am ET on Monday, January 11 and continuing until Friday, January 15 at midnight. Jobs held in a pending state due to intersection with this maintenance window will resume after the maintenance is complete.

Work being completed during this maintenance window is expected to include firmware updates, software updates, rewiring/recabling of some equipment.

If you have questions or need help, please let us know.

Galen

Galen Collier, PhD Director, Research Support Office of Advanced Research Computing (OARC)
