## Impact of Power Outage on the Amarel Cluster

Dear OARC Users,

We had power outages in the Busch campus on many locations. This impacted our data center and caused many nodes to become unavailable. As a result, many nodes are in the down state, and you will see some of your jobs are terminated or waiting in the queue for a long time.

Please check your jobs and be prepared to resubmit them when the cluster comes back to full availability. We will update you when all the compute nodes are fully available.

Thank you, Bala Desinghu, PhD Senior Scientist Office of Advanced Research Computing (OARC)
