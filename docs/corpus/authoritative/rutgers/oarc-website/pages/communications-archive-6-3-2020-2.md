## Clarification to notice of HPCC ownership retirement

Les Michelson Wednesday, June 3, 2020 at 5:56 pm

Dear Amarel owners,

Please accept my apologies for the confusion the term HPCC has apparently caused. HPCC is an older cluster that was integrated into Amarel and is scheduled for retirement on June 1, 2020. This does not impact any Amarel Phase I, II or III purchases. These nodes will remain operational as specified by the SLA applicable to each purchase. Happy computing!

Regards, Les
