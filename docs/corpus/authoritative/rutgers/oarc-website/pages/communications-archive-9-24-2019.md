## Caliburn storage systems are now (temporarily) accessible

Galen Collier Tuesday, September 24, 2019 at 11:39 am

Caliburn Users,

**Access to Storage System:** We've been able to get infrastructure work schedules aligned sufficiently to temporarily enable access to Caliburn's storage system. That system is now available for access via the usual methods (e.g., SFTP). Our team will keep these systems up and accessible between 12:00pm ET Monday and 3:00pm ET Friday this week and next week.

Of course, let us know if you encounter problems accessing or moving your data.

Galen
