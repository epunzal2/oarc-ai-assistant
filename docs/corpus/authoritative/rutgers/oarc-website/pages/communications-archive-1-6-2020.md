## Caliburn Status Update - Expected Full Production by Jan 30.

Paul Arias Monday, January 6, 2020 at 1:30 pm

Good afternoon,

We wanted to take this time to inform you of the plan for Caliburn's return to production.

Between August and the end of September, the technical team at OARC took time to research the system and to prepare a plan of action that would return Caliburn to production. The Module Data Center (MDC) preparation should be complete by mid-January to allow Caliburn to return to full production by January 29, 2020. The high level task schedule appears in the attached png file.

A more detailed view of the MDC Caliburn Readiness Plan can be viewed [here](https://publish.smartsheet.com/dadea77b8129401ab41c348d289d1ad4). On or around January 29, 2020, Caliburn will be available to previous users. Further updates can be viewed at [https://oarc.rutgers.edu/caliburn-system-status/](https://oarc.rutgers.edu/caliburn-system-status/)
