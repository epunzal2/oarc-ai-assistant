## Announcing the Weekly Open Workshop

Galen Collier Wednesday, July 8, 2020 at 5:18 pm

Greetings Everyone,

We're adding a new training and education option for our user community: the **Weekly Open Workshop**

Each week, we'll host a brief Zoom session that provides (1) basics for new Amarel cluster users and (2) shifts into specific technical topics and Q&A. We'll have occasional guest presenters (researchers, librarians, scholars, or other professionals) to share info, news, techniques, or best practices for managing data and computational research. We're also very interested in hearing what topics you'd like to have presented.

These sessions are currently scheduled for 10am-12pm every Thursday starting tomorrow, July 14. See [https://rutgers.box.com/v/weekly-open-workshop](https://rutgers.box.com/v/weekly-open-workshop) for details. Please stop by to watch, listen, or join the conversation.
