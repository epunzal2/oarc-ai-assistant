## OARC ANNOUNCEMENT - December 20, 2021

## Amarel Cluster users,

## Due to an interruption in power in Newark around 12pm, today, December 20, 2021 we experience loss of performance for AmarelN cluster in Newark. Some cluster components are not reachable, i.e. Open On Demand interface and a job scheduler. As a result jobs submitted before 12pm today may be lost and new jobs can't be scheduled at this time.

## We are working to resolve the problem as soon as possible. Meanwhile if you can please try to use amarel.rutgers.edu cluster and ondemand.hpc.rutgers.edu in Piscataway.

## We apologize for inconvenience that it may cause.
