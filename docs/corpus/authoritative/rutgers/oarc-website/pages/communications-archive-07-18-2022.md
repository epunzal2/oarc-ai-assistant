## Amarel Maintenance Tomorrow - Tues, July 19 to Wed, July 20

Amarel Cluster Users,

The monthly maintenance for Amarel will be on Tues. July 19 and Wed. July 20:

*** Start: Tues, July 19, 2022, 8am *** *** End: Wed, July 20, 2022, 7pm ***

The maintenance will include the following hardware and software tasks: Hill Ethernet - reconcile switch uplinks and spanning-tree Configure networking and monitoring for existing Hill Center PDUs Upgrade Lenovo Amarel equipment to Lenovo Best Recipe 22B (https://support.lenovo.com/us/en/solutions/HT513895) Migrate GPFS client cluster configurations to release=LATEST (minReleaseLevel 5.1.2.0) Intermittent H/W problems on amarel[1-2]; physically reseat and expel dust around connector

Operating system patching for CentOS/Debian Infrastructure, 202207 The following systems will be patched: strep chernobog biomech leif2, saul squid arba, kirk wwulfc

A message will be sent to all uses once the maintenance has been completed.
