## Unexpected network problems

Amarel Users,

We are currently encountering unexpected network problems, which are not related to the OARC equipment but are affecting the performance of the Amarel cluster. We have experienced two brief network disconnections in the past two hours, each lasting around 10-15 minutes. As a result, some jobs that were either running or scheduled may have been lost.

Our technical team has been in contact with TD in search of an explanation and a resolution. In the meantime, we are actively monitoring the network connections. Currently, we have no intention of disrupting your workflow by suspending job submission to the Amarel cluster. However, we kindly request that you check your jobs and inform us if you notice any discrepancy. Thank you for your patience and understanding.

Vlad Kholodovych, PhD Director of Research Support Office of Advanced Research Computing (OARC)
