## MOE Site License training sessions

Amarel Users,

Please see announcement about MOE training sessions below and a registration link if you are interested. Because you will be using Amarel cluster in Piscataway for this training you are qualified as RUP users ( Rutgers University Piscataway).

Please note that you are not allowed to use MOE on the cluster in other locations. Let me know if you have any questions. **Vlad Kholodovych, PhD** Director of Research Support Rutgers Office of Advanced Research Computing (OARC)

===================================

I wanted to give you some good news regarding the MOE training sessions for Site License users. You'll be glad to know that we have enhanced the training cycle in two ways:

1. We now offer **a different topic every single month**, instead of every two months as before.
 This way, Rutgers University Piscataway (RUP) students and researchers will have the opportunity
 to learn a more varied range of applications, including AutoPH4 (automatic pharmacophore model
 generation), AMBER TI Integration, Solvent Analysis, among others.
2. We now offer **five different schedules every month**, so that RUP students and researchers can
 sign up to the one that best fits their schedule.

The link to sign up to the training sessions is **[https://calendly.com/moe-site-licensers-2024](https://nam02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcalendly.com%2Fmoe-site-licensers-2024&data=05%7C02%7Cjrb267%40oarc.rutgers.edu%7C72e3d16420354ca4503a08dc21cbe7c1%7Cb92d2b234d35447093ff69aca6632ffe%7C1%7C0%7C638422406848461919%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=OS6xT90ISJQuaA275ALnG6Yv9BgDajf5nQ7vF72b2DU%3D&reserved=0)**; could you please share it with as many RUP students and researchers as possible?

If at any point you would like me to organize a training session on a specific topic, please let me know.

We will continue to offer our monthly open training session for new users. Our February session will be on Wednesday, February 14th, at 10 am EST. Would you, or any other member of your team, like to join us? Should you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact me.
