## OARC Holiday Support Schedule

Eric Marshall Thursday, December 21, 2017 at 4:50 pm

The Office of Advanced Research Computing will be closed for most normal business during the week of 12/25 - 1/1 in accordance with the University holiday calendar. All remote services will be operational and monitored along with our support line, [help@hpc.rutgers.edu](mailto:help@hpc.rutgers.edu) .

As a result of reduced workforce during this week, effort will be focused if necessary on problems associated with our technical infrastructure. For this reason, you should expect that hardware and access problems will be given a higher priority. Requests for new application and scientific support should be deferred to the following week whenever possible as a timely response cannot be guaranteed during the holiday week. Thank you for your cooperation and best wishes for a happy and healthy holiday season.

**The OARC System Administration and Scientific Support Teams** - Leslie P. Michelson and the rest of the OARC
