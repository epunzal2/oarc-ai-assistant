## Young Researchers: Apply for the 11th Heidelberg Laureate Forum, September 22 - 27, 2024

Good afternoon!

Please see the forwarded announcement below. OARC would like to share this amazing opportunity for undergraduates, graduate students, or recent PhDs:

**The application process has opened for the 11th Heidelberg Laureate Forum Foundation (HLFF), which will be held September 22-27, 2024, in Heidelberg, Germany.**The Heidelberg Laureate Forum is a wonderful opportunity for young researchers to meet and interact with Laureates from computing (recipients of the Turing Award and ACM Prize in Computing) and mathematics (Abel Prize, Fields Medal, and IMU Abacus Medal).

Detailed information is included below. To learn more and apply here: [https://www.heidelberg-laureate-forum.org/](https://nam02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.heidelberg-laureate-forum.org%2F&data=05%7C01%7Cjrb267%40oarc.rutgers.edu%7C2db902cc9f4c47153de808dbf04b5211%7Cb92d2b234d35447093ff69aca6632ffe%7C1%7C0%7C638367978517304444%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=d6Tdz2XlxOILUcylYMJFhEloYB%2FVGLWWamFyeFtE2L4%3D&reserved=0). Applications must be submitted by Friday, February 9, 2024. The HLFF willbook and cover the costs for a single hotel accommodation(including breakfast). Travel costs are **not** automatically covered by the HLFF.

Please feel dree to share this message with anyone who may be interested. Let us know if you have any additional questions. Thank you!

**Janae Baker, MLER**Senior Program Coordinator Office of Advanced Research Computing ([OARC](http://oarc.rutgers.edu/))

Turing Award and the ACM Prize in Computing (Computer Science), and the Abel Prize, Fields Medal, and IMU Abacus Medal (Mathematics) will engage in a cross-generational scientific dialogue with young researchers in Heidelberg, Germany.
