## Date change for Amarel Maintenance in January

Amarel Users,

We realized that the current date of Amarel maintenance scheduled in January is too close to the beginning of the year and decided to shift it two weeks later, from January 10-11, 2023 **to January 24-25, 2023**. It will provide time for currently running jobs to finish before the next maintenance.

Please let us know asap if it may lead to any problem for your schedule. Sorry for inconvenience that it may cause.
