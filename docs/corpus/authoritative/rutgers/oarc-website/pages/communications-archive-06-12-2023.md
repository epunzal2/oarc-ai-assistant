## OARC hiring - senior scientist/research facilitator

The Office of Advanced Research Computing (OARC) is seeking a Senior Scientist/Research Facilitator to join our growing team.

In this role, you will support the Rutgers research community facing complex computational projects and enable them to achieve their research goals.

This position reports to the Director of Research Support, under the Associate Vice President for Advanced Research Computing. If you have a strong foundation in chemistry, genomics, physics, biology, clinical sciences, life sciences, or any other research discipline, and you're interested in collaborating with Rutgers faculty, post-doctoral associates, students, or research staff to advance their research to the next level, then this is an exciting opportunity for you!

Learn more about the position and consider applying here: [https://jobs.rutgers.edu/postings/201612](https://jobs.rutgers.edu/postings/201612).

Vlad Kholodovych, PhD Interim Director, Research Support / Senior Scientist
