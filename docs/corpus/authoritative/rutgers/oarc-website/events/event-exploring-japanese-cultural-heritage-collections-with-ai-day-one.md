## Event Description

What hidden voices could be found in archival collections using Artificial Intelligence (AI)? Who else's stories could be told?

 Rutgers University and Durham University UK will be holding two joint workshops to investigate how AI can be used to help us make sense of our archival collections, especially those that relate to Japan in Meiji Period (the late nineteenth century). Both events are in-person only.

 The Rutgers workshops, held September 15-16, are led by Special Collections and University Archives (SC/UA) and the Office of Advanced Research Computing (OARC) in association with the department of Asian Languages and Cultures.
