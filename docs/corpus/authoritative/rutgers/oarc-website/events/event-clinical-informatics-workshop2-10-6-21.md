## Event Description

**Workshop 2: Computer science in clinical medicine, introduction into Machine learning, Advanced machine learning methods in medicine - review** Branimir Ljubic presents the second workshop within the series of workshops: "Clinical informatics, Data science in medicine, Advanced computing."
