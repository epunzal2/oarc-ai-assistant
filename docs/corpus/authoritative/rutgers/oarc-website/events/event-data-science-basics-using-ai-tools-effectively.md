## Event Description

Artificial Intelligence is already part of your daily life, from smart assistants to content recommendations, but how can you use it for your own benefit? This beginner-friendly workshop explores free, web-based AI tools that help with writing, design, productivity, and more. You'll experiment with different tools in real time and discover how AI can save time, spark ideas, and make your work easier. No prior knowledge or installations are required, just curiosity and an open mind.
