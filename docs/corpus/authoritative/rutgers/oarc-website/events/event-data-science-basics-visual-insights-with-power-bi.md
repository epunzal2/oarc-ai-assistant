## Event Description

Numbers alone rarely tell the whole story. In this workshop, you'll discover how to bring data to life through powerful visuals and interactive dashboards. With Power BI, you'll learn how to create charts and reports that make complex information clear, professional, and engaging. Whether you're presenting at work, showing results in class, or simply trying to make sense of personal data, this workshop will give you the ability to communicate with impact.
