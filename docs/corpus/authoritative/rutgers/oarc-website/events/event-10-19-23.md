## Event Description

Are you interested in exploring and analyzing historical or cultural networks representing trade, communication, kinship relationships, or similar? In this workshop, we will use [Gephi](https://gephi.org/) to analyze the relationships between actors and make a network graph to show our findings. No prior experience with network analysis is required.
