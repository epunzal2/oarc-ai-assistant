# Slurm Team

Slurm development has been a joint effort of many companies and organizations around the world. Over
200 individuals have contributed to Slurm.

Slurm development is lead by [SchedMD](https://www.schedmd.com). It's staff of developers and
support personnel maintain the canonical Slurm releases, and are responsible for the majority of the
development work for each new Slurm release.
